# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — October 2026

ReadScore now helps you read the page itself instead of scoring it.

- **Focus** highlights one paragraph at a time. Move with `J` and `K` or let it follow your scroll.
- **Comfort** adjusts text size, font and spacing. Atkinson Hyperlegible is included.
- **Calm** reduces motion, pauses background videos and blurs large images until you hover over them.
- The three modes can be used together, and your settings sync across your Chrome browsers.
- New popup design and icon.
- The readability score, Selection Analysis and AI Summary have been removed.
- Requires Chrome 116 or newer. Permissions are unchanged.
